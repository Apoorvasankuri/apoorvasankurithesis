## Why this sector

India's infrastructure capex cycle — National Infrastructure Pipeline, PM Gati Shakti, renewable energy targets, railway electrification — is creating a multi-year demand tailwind across power T&D, renewables, railways, and civil infrastructure. Unlike the AI-healthcare thesis, the investable edge here is not a technology moat but **execution discipline**: order book quality, working capital management, and the ability to consolidate a fragmented contractor base.

## Scoring framework (replaces "AI maturity" from the healthcare thesis)

Each sub-sector is evaluated on:

1. **Order book & revenue visibility** — share of revenue locked into multi-year EPC contracts vs. spot/tender-based work
2. **Working capital intensity** — receivables, retention money, and mobilization advance cycles; EPC businesses are structurally working-capital-heavy
3. **Government/policy dependency** — reliance on government capex cycles (PLI schemes, NIP, PM Gati Shakti) vs. private capex
4. **Margin structure & execution risk** — fixed-price contract exposure, cost-overrun risk, input price volatility (steel, copper, aluminum)
5. **Consolidation potential** — fragmented and roll-up-ready, or already concentrated among a few large players
